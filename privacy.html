Ledger Rush

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

Ledger Rush is a free study site for intermediate accounting. This page explains what information it collects and how it's used.

Without signing in

You can use every study mode without an account. Your progress, settings and study time are saved only in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device.

When you sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in, Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with Ledger Rush. We use them only to create your account and keep you signed in. We also store:

  • The display name you choose for the leaderboard.
  • Your study progress and settings, so they sync across your devices.
  • How many seconds you studied each chapter each day, which powers the leaderboard.

What's public

The leaderboard shows your chosen display name and your total study time. Your email address, your Google name and your detailed progress are never shown to other users.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. The site is hosted on Vercel. We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your information with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Signing out removes your progress from that browser. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email the address below and it will be removed.
